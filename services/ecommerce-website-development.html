Why Direct Online Catalogs Beat Marketplace Dependance

In regions like Varanasi (famous for Banarasi silk and handloom sarees) and Mirzapur (famous for handcrafted carpets and brassware), local business owners often struggle with high commissions on marketplace platforms.

Having your own branded online store lets you present your unique catalog, set custom pricing, and collect orders directly from customers across India with zero marketplace cuts.

How Direct WhatsApp Ordering Works

For most Indian buyers, direct WhatsApp checkout converts 3x higher than complicated account registrations:

  • 1. Product Selection: The buyer browses your collection on their smartphone.
  • 2. One-Tap Order: Clicking "Order on WhatsApp" opens their WhatsApp app with product title, color/variant, quantity, and price pre-written.
  • 3. Direct Finalization: You confirm shipping address and accept payment via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) or Cash on Delivery.
