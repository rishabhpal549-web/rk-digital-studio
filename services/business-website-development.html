Our 4-Step Development Process

We believe in straightforward, reliable timelines without unnecessary delays:

  • 1. Discovery & Structure Plan (Day 1): We discuss your business type, target clients, preferred color palette, and required pages.
  • 2. UI & Content Architecture (Days 2–3): We draft clean layouts with clear typography, high-contrast buttons, and natural English or bilingual content.
  • 3. Responsive Code & Form Integration (Days 4–5): Full HTML5/CSS development with WhatsApp routing, contact validation, and cross-browser testing.
  • 4. Launch, SEO Check & Handover (Day 6): Domain setup, Google Search Console sitemap submission, and complete deployment on Vercel or your hosting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a business website?
A standard 5-to-7 section business website typically takes between 4 to 7 working days from receiving your business details to final live deployment.
Will my website show up on Google search results?
Yes. We implement technical SEO including descriptive meta title tags, meta descriptions, semantic heading hierarchy (H1, H2), canonical tags, and submit your XML sitemap directly to Google Search Console for indexing.
Can I update my phone number or services later?
Absolutely. We structure the code cleanly and provide post-launch support so you can update contact numbers, services, or pricing whenever your business grows.
